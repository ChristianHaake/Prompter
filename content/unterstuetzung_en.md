# Support Prompter

Prompter is free to use. Contributions are voluntary and do not unlock extra features.

## What contributions support

Contributions support Prompter's maintenance and development, including bug fixes, testing, and accessibility improvements. The application is hosted as a static website on Cloudflare.

## Contribute

[Support Prompter through Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/Haake)

This link opens an external provider. Prompter does not load the provider's content, scripts, or tracking pixels before you open the link. The provider's [privacy policy](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy) applies after you leave the app.

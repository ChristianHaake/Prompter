## Service

Prompter is a free browser application for writing and presenting scripts. It does not require an account.

## Your content

Scripts entered in the app remain in the browser. The current draft is stored locally there. Project and CSV files are created only when the user exports them. Opening a project can replace the current draft; the app asks before doing so.

Users decide which content they enter, export or share. Sensitive personal data should not be used in classroom scripts.

## Availability and contact

The app requires a current browser. Browser features such as fullscreen or Wake Lock may be unavailable. Questions and bug reports can be sent to [apps@haak3.de](mailto:apps@haak3.de); do not include private script content.
